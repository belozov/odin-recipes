Fluffy Pancakes

Description

These pancakes are thick, soft, and airy, with golden edges and a light vanilla flavor. They come together in minutes from simple pantry staples and are ideal for a relaxed weekend breakfast.

Serve them stacked with butter and maple syrup, or top them with fresh berries, banana slices, or a spoonful of yogurt. The batter can be made quickly, and leftover pancakes reheat well in a toaster.

Ingredients

  • 200 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 250 ml milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter, plus extra for the pan
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Maple syrup and fresh berries, for serving

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract until combined.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently until just combined. A few small lumps are fine; do not overmix.
  4. Let the batter rest for 5–10 minutes while you heat the pan.
  5. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat and lightly grease it with butter.
  6. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the pan, leaving space between them.
  7. Cook for 2–3 minutes, until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set.
  8. Flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes until golden brown on the second side.
  9. Keep finished pancakes warm in a 90°C (200°F) oven while you cook the rest. Serve with maple syrup and berries.
