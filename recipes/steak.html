Pan-Seared Steak

Description

A perfectly pan-seared steak has a deep golden-brown crust and a juicy, tender center. Basting with garlic and herb butter at the end adds a rich, aromatic finish that makes it taste like it came from a steakhouse.

This recipe needs only a handful of ingredients and about 20 minutes. The key is a very hot pan, a well-dried steak, and a short rest before slicing, so all the juices stay inside the meat.

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye or striploin steaks, about 250 g each and 3 cm thick
  • 1 tbsp vegetable or avocado oil
  • 1 tsp coarse sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 40 g butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 3 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 sprig fresh rosemary

Steps

  1. Take the steaks out of the fridge 30–40 minutes before cooking so they come to room temperature.
  2. Pat the steaks very dry with paper towels, then season generously on both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat a heavy cast-iron or stainless steel skillet over high heat for 3–4 minutes until it is very hot. Add the oil.
  4. Place the steaks in the pan and sear for 3–4 minutes without moving them, until a deep brown crust forms.
  5. Flip the steaks and cook for another 2 minutes. Reduce the heat to medium.
  6. Add the butter, garlic, thyme, and rosemary. Tilt the pan and spoon the foaming butter over the steaks for 1–2 minutes.
  7. Check doneness: about 52°C (125°F) for medium-rare, 57°C (135°F) for medium, 63°C (145°F) for medium-well.
  8. Transfer the steaks to a plate and let them rest for 5–8 minutes, loosely covered with foil.
  9. Slice against the grain, spoon over the pan butter, and serve right away.
