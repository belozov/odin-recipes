Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made of wide pasta sheets layered with rich meat ragù, creamy béchamel sauce, and melted cheese. Each layer bakes into a tender, hearty slice with a golden, bubbly crust on top.

The combination of savory tomato-meat sauce, silky béchamel, and stretchy mozzarella with a touch of Parmesan makes lasagna the ultimate comfort food. It is perfect for family dinners and holiday tables, and it tastes even better the next day.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 500 g ground beef (or a beef and pork mix)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 carrot, finely grated
  • 400 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g all-purpose flour
  • 600 ml whole milk
  • A pinch of nutmeg
  • 250 g mozzarella, shredded
  • 80 g Parmesan, grated

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and garlic and cook for 5–7 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned, about 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer on low heat for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens.
  4. Prepare the béchamel: melt the butter in a saucepan, whisk in the flour, and cook for 1–2 minutes without browning.
  5. Gradually pour in the milk, whisking constantly, and cook for 5–7 minutes until thick and smooth. Season with salt and nutmeg.
  6. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  7. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish. Cover with 3–4 lasagna sheets, then add a layer of meat sauce, a layer of béchamel, and a sprinkle of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  8. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with béchamel and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  9. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  10. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing so the layers set. Serve warm.
